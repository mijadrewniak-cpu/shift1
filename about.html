Our Story

SHIFT Healing Center was created to make thoughtful mental health care easier to access and less fragmented.

People are often expected to identify the right service themselves, contact several separate practices, repeat their story to different professionals, and coordinate their own care while already struggling.

SHIFT offers a more connected experience. By bringing therapy, psychiatric care, and addiction recovery support together within one practice, we can help clients find an appropriate provider and access additional support if their needs change.

Our goal is to make the process feel clearer, more personal, and easier to navigate from the first conversation onward.

Our Approach

We Begin With You—not Just a Diagnosis

Two people can experience similar symptoms for completely different reasons. Anxiety, for example, may be connected to trauma, relationship insecurity, chronic stress, perfectionism, physical symptoms, or a major life change.

That is why care at SHIFT begins with understanding what you are experiencing, how it affects your life, what may be contributing to it, and what you want to change.

Your history, relationships, culture, identity, strengths, preferences, boundaries, and comfort level all help shape your treatment.

Care at SHIFT is:

  • Evidence-based: We use therapeutic methods supported by clinical research and professional experience.
  • Individualized: Treatment is selected and adapted around your specific concerns and goals.
  • Collaborative: You are involved in setting goals and discussing what feels helpful.
  • Responsive: Your treatment can change as your circumstances, progress, and needs change.
  • Whole-person focused: We consider emotional, behavioral, relational, physical, and environmental factors—not symptoms in isolation.

You do not need to know which therapy approach or service to choose before contacting us. We will help you understand the available options and identify an appropriate next step.

Why SHIFT

Connected Care Without Starting Over

Some people need therapy alone. Others may benefit from psychiatric evaluation or medication support alongside therapy. Someone seeking addiction recovery care may also need help with trauma, depression, anxiety, or relationship difficulties.

SHIFT brings multiple forms of support together within one practice:

  • Individual therapy
  • Couples and family therapy
  • Psychiatric evaluation and medication management
  • Addiction and recovery support
  • Trauma-focused treatment
  • Emotional regulation and nervous-system support

When more than one service is appropriate, your providers may coordinate your care with your permission. This can improve communication, create a clearer treatment plan, and reduce the need to repeatedly explain your history to unrelated practices.

You will not automatically be placed into every service. We help determine which care is relevant to your situation and adjust the plan when necessary.

Schedule an Intake Session

After your inquiry, we will help schedule a comprehensive intake session with an appropriate licensed provider or psychiatric professional.

During this first session, you will have an opportunity to:

  • Share your mental health or substance use concerns
  • Discuss how those concerns affect your daily life
  • Explain what you would like to change or understand
  • Begin creating a personalized treatment plan
  • Learn about your recommended therapy, psychiatric care, or recovery support
  • Ask questions and understand what to expect from ongoing sessions

The intake session allows your provider to understand your needs before recommending the next step. It is also an opportunity for you to learn about the provider and decide whether the relationship feels comfortable and appropriate.

Sessions are virtual, secure, and HIPAA-compliant, allowing you to access care while physically located anywhere in California.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer virtual care throughout California?

Yes. SHIFT offers secure virtual appointments to clients who are physically located in California at the time of service. Availability may vary depending on the provider and type of care required.

Do you offer in-person appointments?

Please contact our care coordination team to ask whether any providers currently offer in-person appointments and whether this option is appropriate for your needs.

What types of care does SHIFT provide?

SHIFT brings therapy, psychiatric care, and addiction recovery support together within one practice. Available services include individual therapy, couples and family therapy, psychiatric evaluation, medication management, trauma-focused treatment, and recovery support.

Do I need to know which service I need?

No. You do not need to diagnose yourself or select a treatment before contacting us. Tell us what you have been experiencing, and we will help you identify an appropriate provider and next step.

How do I choose the right provider?

You do not have to choose alone. We consider your concerns, goals, preferences, scheduling needs, and what matters to you in a provider. We then help connect you with a clinician whose expertise and therapeutic style may be a good fit.

What happens during the first appointment?

Your first appointment is an opportunity to discuss your concerns, relevant history, current needs, and goals. Your provider will ask questions, explain possible treatment options, and begin developing a personalized plan with you.

Do you provide both therapy and psychiatric care?

Yes. SHIFT offers therapy and psychiatric services. Depending on your needs, you may receive one service or work with more than one provider as part of a coordinated treatment plan.

Does receiving psychiatric care mean I must take medication?

No. A psychiatric provider can evaluate your symptoms, discuss possible options, and answer your questions. Medication is not automatically prescribed, and decisions about treatment should be made collaboratively with your provider.

How long does therapy last?

Every client’s experience is different. Some people seek short-term support for a specific concern, while others choose longer-term therapy for trauma recovery, relationship work, recurring patterns, or continued personal growth. Your therapist will review your progress and goals with you so that treatment continues to reflect your needs.

How often will I meet with my therapist?

Many clients begin with weekly sessions to create consistency and build momentum. The frequency may later change depending on your goals, progress, availability, and clinical needs.

Can my treatment change over time?

Yes. Your provider may adjust your goals, therapy approach, session frequency, or recommended services as your needs change. Treatment should remain relevant to what you are experiencing rather than follow a rigid formula.

Do you accept insurance?

Insurance participation varies by provider and service. Contact our care coordination team with your insurance information so we can help you understand available options. Coverage should always be confirmed before beginning treatment.

What if my provider does not feel like the right match?

A strong provider relationship is an important part of effective care. If the match does not feel right, let us know. When possible, we can help you explore another provider or treatment option.

Crisis Resources

SHIFT Healing Center provides outpatient mental health services and is not a crisis or emergency service.

If you are experiencing a mental health emergency or believe you may be at immediate risk of harming yourself or someone else:

  • Call 911
  • Go to your nearest emergency department
  • Call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
  • Text HOME to 741741 to contact the Crisis Text Line

Please do not use email, voicemail, or the website contact form for urgent or emergency situations.
