Crisis Resources

If you need urgent support, you can contact:

  • 988: Call or text the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
  • 741741: Text HOME to the Crisis Text Line.
  • 911: Call for an immediate emergency.

SHIFT’s website and contact forms are not monitored for emergencies.
