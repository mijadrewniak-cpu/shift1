Why SHIFT

Different Needs, One Connected Practice

Mental health concerns often overlap. Someone beginning therapy may also benefit from psychiatric support, while someone in recovery may need help with trauma, anxiety, depression, or relationships.

SHIFT makes these services available within one practice. When appropriate and with your permission, providers may coordinate care—creating a clearer treatment experience without requiring you to navigate several unrelated practices alone.

  • Experienced, licensed professionals
  • Evidence-based treatment
  • Personalized care planning
  • Trauma-informed support
  • Secure virtual appointments
  • Support that can adapt as your needs change
